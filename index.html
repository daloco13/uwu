Guide ▶

  • 1. Visit https://uwu-logs.xyz/top?server=Icecrown
  • 2. Click on "Top", select a class and select a player
  • 3. Choose a boss and click on the "Date" (e.g. 13-03-24)
  • 4. Click on the player, on the upper right, click on the icon and select a boss (e.g. Deathbringer)
  • 5. Click on "Timeline"
  • 6. Right click on the page, then find "Inspect element", find "<spells-main class="casts-section-main>" and right click > Copy > Element or Inner HTML
  • 7. Go to spell timeline site, paste the result in "Combat Log", click on button ("Display Log")
